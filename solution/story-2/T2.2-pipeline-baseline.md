## 9. Observations

### Actual Timing Pattern

- The N=10 successful CI sample shows elapsed duration between `13:20` and `13:57`.
- The N=10 successful CI timing baseline is captured and provides the measured before-state for CI elapsed duration.
- The N=10 average CI duration is `13:35`; median is `13:31`.
- `Command Adaptor` and `Integration Tests` remain the largest visible cost concentration areas.
- Because visible step durations may overlap, they should be treated as cost concentration indicators rather than directly removable elapsed time.
- `Kafka & Redis` is a consistent smaller cost area at around `02:07-02:33`.
- `mvn clean install` is around `01:13-01:49` in this sample.
- The current sample mixes feature, develop and release branches; branch-specific baselines can be separated later if required.
- The manually reviewed failed/unsuccessful-build sample provides a useful reliability baseline signal.
- Visible failure concentration appears around `mvn clean install` and `Kafka & Redis`, with additional release publish/CD failures.
- These are visible failed stages, not confirmed root causes.
- No optimisation savings are claimed yet; this document establishes the before-state for later comparison.

### Duplicate Maven Build

The pipeline shape still shows a duplicate Maven-build risk: `mvn clean install` appears in the CI flow, and the integration-test container also runs `mvn -Pci-snapshot install` in the broader compose/test model. This remains a structural observation only.

Build-once/promote-once should be investigated later as a possible improvement. It is not part of the baseline capture task, and no savings should be attached to it until measured.

### Pipeline Caching

- No repo-visible Maven dependency cache between Drone runs was identified. Dependency resolution may still benefit from Artifactory/proxy caching outside this pipeline.
- No repo-visible Docker layer cache between runs was identified because DIND is ephemeral. Any platform-level caching would need confirmation.
- Do not claim caching-related optimisation savings until the platform behaviour is confirmed and before/after data exists.
