## 1. Executive Summary

T3.2 added and validated a targeted `.dockerignore` under `cmd-adaptor-sns`. The Docker build completed successfully and Docker loaded the `.dockerignore`. The observed build context transfer dropped from the T2.3 reference baseline of `191.27MB` to `189B`. The Dockerfile `COPY` steps for both required runtime artefacts completed successfully, and a runtime artefact check confirmed that `cmd-adaptor-sns-exec.jar` and `opentelemetry-javaagent.jar` are present in the final image.

The targeted `.dockerignore` was validated successfully in the SNS repository. For durable reuse and consistency across command-adaptor repositories, `.dockerignore` ownership and distribution should be considered through RepoSync. RepoSync adoption is a recommended follow-up.

The final image size remained unchanged at `906MB`. Cold build time remained broadly similar at `real 78.14s` because the dominant build cost remains the `yum`/install/`envconsul` layer, observed at `72.4s` in the T3.2 build log. No CI saving, image-size saving, or cold-build-time improvement is claimed.
