## Adoption constraints

- CI benefit requires measurement in the relevant CI environment.
- A reduced service set must be validated against an equivalent full-E2E scope.
- Redis-only results cannot justify Kafka/Schema Registry or broad infrastructure migration.
- Reliability/flaky-test improvement requires repeated rerun evidence.
- A temporary target-repository change remains temporary until the durable owner accepts it.
- No default pipeline, production or multi-repository rollout proceeds from prepared materials alone.
