## Ownership categories

### CST-local

- Maintain the measured evidence and its limitations.
- Retain or refine the opt-in Redis pilot without changing default CI or full E2E behaviour.
- Perform authorised target-repository experiments and equivalent local validation.
- Prepare proposals for centrally owned changes.

Temporary repository work does not establish durable ownership when RepoSync or another platform process generates or controls the file.

### RepoSync / platform

- Decide the durable `.dockerignore` distribution route where central management applies.
- Review the layer-order candidate and measure CI applicability before any production/default adoption.
- Decide any `.drone.star`, DIND, CI environment, Compose-template or single-orchestrator change.
- Decide build-once-promote and any cache infrastructure needed by CI.

Technical feasibility and a successful local prototype do not authorise these changes.

### Wider ETO / enabling

- Shared base-image lifecycle and patching policy.
- Cross-repository Testcontainers libraries or templates.
- Remote-cache infrastructure beyond a repository-specific mechanism.
- Organisation-wide scanning, signing or supply-chain standards.

These are considerations for prioritisation, not approved backlog commitments.

## Evidence boundaries

### Measured

- `.dockerignore` build-context result and artefact preservation.
- Layer-order local same-daemon JAR-change warm-cache result.
- Redis Testcontainers opt-in local functional runs.
- Redis-only Compose functional operations and cleanup time, where recorded by Story 4.

### Observed / structural

- Repository ownership markers and pipeline/configuration topology.
- Compose service definitions and mapped invocation paths.
- Separate Drone and Maven orchestration paths.

### Inferred / proposed

- CI time saving from any local result.
- Benefit from reducing Compose services.
- Kafka/Schema Registry migration.
- Broad command-adaptor reuse.
- Shared platform patterns.

These inferred items need their own implementation and measurement before they become validated outcomes.

## Explicit non-claims

- CI saving: `not measured`.
- Docker layer-order image-size improvement: not demonstrated.
- Docker layer-order cold-build improvement: not demonstrated.
- Flaky-test improvement: `not measured`.
- Reduced-Compose full-E2E result: `not measured`.
- Kafka/Schema Registry Testcontainers result: `not measured`.
- Production or default CI adoption: not implemented.
- Broad command-adaptor rollout: not implemented.
- RepoSync/platform or stakeholder approval: not recorded.

## Priority recommendation

1. Preserve the validated `.dockerignore` evidence and confirm its durable owner.
2. Keep the layer-order result as a RepoSync/platform candidate; do not translate it into a CI claim.
3. Close the current Redis-only Story 4 comparison on functional evidence unless equivalent startup/readiness timing is specifically required.
4. Decide the orchestration and Compose ownership model before proposing a central cutover.
5. Consider wider reusable patterns only after repository-level evidence and owner decisions.
