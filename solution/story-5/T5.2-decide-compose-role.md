## 1. Decision Summary

```text
Current full-E2E path:
  Keep the currently validated Compose mechanism.

Narrow isolated dependency tests:
  Keep Redis Testcontainers Option A as local and opt-in.

Immediate Compose reduction:
  Do not adopt from current evidence.

Validation candidates:
  Kafdrop readiness, Jaeger, LocalStack/wait helper,
  aggregate-v1id-v2id topology divergence, aggregate-matching
  necessity/readiness, and kafka-rest owner use.

Architectural candidates:
  Integration-test runner ownership and overlapping Drone/Maven orchestration.

Durable change:
  Requires equivalent functional evidence plus the relevant
  RepoSync/platform/technical-owner route.
```

No Compose, Maven, Drone, readiness, CI or production change is implemented or approved by this decision.

## 3. Target-Role Principles

1. Preserve a currently validated path until an equivalent replacement or reduction is functionally validated.
2. Treat `CANDIDATE` as a recommendation for separately scoped and approved investigation, not approval to implement or adopt.
3. Keep CI, full-E2E, local full-stack, local utility and narrow dependency-test scopes separate.
4. Do not infer performance, reliability or developer-feedback benefits from structural analysis.
5. Resolve technical ownership and the durable RepoSync/platform route before changing centrally managed behaviour.
6. Decide the intended orchestration owner before designing broad Compose reduction.

Decision categories:

- `KEEP — current validated path`
- `CANDIDATE — requires isolated validation`
- `STOP / NOT NOW — unsupported by current evidence`
- `LOCAL UTILITY — preserve outside automated CI scope`
- `ARCHITECTURAL CANDIDATE — requires design and ownership decision`

## 7. Architectural and Ownership Candidates

The Drone and Maven paths are overlapping, not proven exact duplicates:

- Drone owns CI startup, readiness, test-container execution and DIND lifecycle.
- Maven owns local profile startup, readiness and teardown.
- The paths use different service sets and teardown behaviour.
- No single-orchestrator model has been implemented or validated.

Target direction:

1. Define the intended owner for startup, readiness, test execution and teardown.
2. Validate the target model locally and in CI before reducing Compose scope.
3. Route durable changes to RepoSync-controlled Compose/readiness/pipeline files through the owning platform process.
4. Keep repo-local experiments explicitly temporary until the durable owner adopts them.

---

## 8. Evidence Required Before Change

Every candidate change needs evidence proportional to its affected scope:

- exact configuration or code change under test
- equivalent functional E2E runs when current services/readiness are affected
- CI execution when a CI role or saving is claimed
- separate startup/readiness timing only when performance is decision-relevant
- repeated-run evidence before reliability or flaky-test claims
- local workflow validation when developer tooling changes
- owner confirmation for undocumented/manual use
- rollback route and current-behaviour comparison
- RepoSync/platform decision for durable centrally managed changes

Redis-only evidence cannot substitute for Kafka, Schema Registry, custom-application or full-E2E evidence.
